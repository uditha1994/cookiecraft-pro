# CookieCraft Pro - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [2026-02-02]

## Overview

CookieCraft Pro is a browser extension for managing cookies and browsing data. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

### What We Access

CookieCraft Pro accesses the following data to provide its functionality:

- **Cookies:** Read, display, modify, and delete browser cookies
- **Browsing Data:** Clear cache, history, and download history when requested
- **Tab Information:** Current tab URL and favicon for displaying site-specific information

### What We Store Locally

The following data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

- User preferences and settings
- Scheduled cleanup configurations
- Cookie statistics and history counts
- Backup metadata

## Data Usage

All data accessed by CookieCraft Pro is:

- Used **exclusively** for extension functionality
- Stored **locally** on your device only
- **Never transmitted** to external servers
- **Never sold** or shared with third parties
- **Never used** for advertising or tracking

## Data Sharing

**We do not share any data with third parties.**

CookieCraft Pro:
- Does not have external servers
- Does not collect analytics
- Does not use tracking pixels
- Does not transmit any user data

## User Control

Users have complete control over their data:

- View all cookies stored in the browser
- Delete any cookies at any time
- Clear browsing data on demand
- Configure or disable scheduled cleanups
- Disable notifications
- Export/backup cookies locally
- Uninstall the extension to remove all stored settings

## Data Security

All data remains within your browser and is protected by Chrome's built-in security mechanisms. No data is transmitted over the internet by this extension.

## Children's Privacy

CookieCraft Pro does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date.
